Component · two presets

One shader · two surfaces · no dependencies

01 — Glow

Bare mode: no glass body, no lens disc, no backdrop sample. Only the light, on transparency. Three soft CSS bands expand behind it — gradients with no edge, never stroked circles.

02 — Button

Glass body, ring, and a second glass lens on top at 54% radius. The lens dims and scatters the light inside it, so the glow reads as coming from underneath rather than sitting on the surface.
